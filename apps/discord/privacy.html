Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

Thick Shift 3D is a co-op game you play in a browser or as a Discord Activity. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies. This page lists everything the game handles and where it goes.

Your Discord name

When you start the Activity in Discord, the game asks Discord for the identify permission. If you allow it, the game's server trades the one-time code Discord gives it for an access token and hands that token straight back to your game. The server doesn't store the token. Your game uses it once to read your Discord display name, and it shows that name to the other players in your room as your guard's name.

The game doesn't read your email, your servers, your friends or your messages, and it doesn't keep your Discord user ID. If you decline, you play as "Guard".

Rooms

Players in one room connect to a server on Cloudflare that runs the night. Your game sends it where you are, what you used and your name, and it sends everyone in the room the state of the night, which includes each guard's name and where they are. It saves that state so a night survives the server restarting, and deletes it 5 minutes after the last player leaves. It knows you by a random player ID your game made up, not by your Discord account. The server's logs record the web address of each connection, which includes the room and that player ID, and Cloudflare keeps those logs for up to 7 days. Inside Discord, the room is named after the Activity's instance ID, which Discord shares with everyone who joins that Activity in the same channel.

On your device

The game saves the name you typed (on the web version) and your control settings in your browser's local storage. That stays on your device. Clear your site data to remove it.

Cloudflare and Discord

Cloudflare hosts the game, so it sees your IP address and the requests your browser makes, as any web host does. See Cloudflare's privacy policy. Inside Discord, Discord's own privacy policy covers what Discord does.

The page's fonts come from Google Fonts and its stylesheets from jsdelivr. On the web your browser fetches them itself, so those services see your IP address. Inside Discord the game's server fetches them for you and passes on only your browser's user agent.

Children

The game is meant for people old enough to use Discord under its terms. It doesn't knowingly collect anything about anyone younger.

Changes and contact

If the game starts handling anything new, this page will say so and the date above will change. Questions or requests go to me@balajileninrajan.dev.
