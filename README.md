# Thick Shift

Co-op night-shift horror in first person. You're a guard at the Mascot Depot. Walk the building with a torch, watch the security cameras, fix the wiring before it burns, crank the generator, and keep the very large retired mascots off you until 6 AM. Survive five nights.

Play alone or with friends, in a browser or in a Discord voice channel.

## Play in a browser

Go to [thickshift.balajileninrajan.dev](https://thickshift.balajileninrajan.dev). Press **Host a night** and send your friends the link or the four-letter room code from the lobby. Start the night on your own to play solo.

## Play in Discord

1. [Add Thick Shift to Discord](https://discord.com/oauth2/authorize?client_id=1572848365811663080). Add it to a server you manage, or to your own account if you don't manage one.
2. Join a voice channel, press the rocket (Activities) button and pick Thick Shift.
3. Everyone in the channel who opens the Activity lands in the same room. Press **Clock in**.

On a phone, Thick Shift runs in landscape with touch controls. A gamepad works too. The controls are on the pause screen (Esc).
